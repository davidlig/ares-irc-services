S2. TOKEN registration: `REGISTER <password> [email]`; optional email is format/uniqueness validated and stored, never an alternative recovery factor. Persist a Pending account with its registration mode but no rescue hash. Privately deliver a CSPRNG UUIDv4 and `VERIFY <token>` instructions/warning. Keep only its SHA-256 hash in temporary memory (3600s TTL capped by pending expiration). Successful VERIFY atomically persists the same hash and activates the account. No reissue, including repeated REGISTER: after token loss, expiry or daemon restart, wait for account expiration before registering anew.
S3. EMAIL registration: `REGISTER <password> <email>`; persist pending account and rescue hash, mail original token, activate through VERIFY without consuming the persistent credential. Pending expiration remains separate. Retain retryable pending state on mail failure. Persist pending delivery mode; preexisting pending accounts are EMAIL. Changing global mode does not change pending account rules, except RESEND's global gate.
